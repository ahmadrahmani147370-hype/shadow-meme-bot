# v1.1.3 - stop loss 20% (13 Nov 2026)

Ahmed: *"Stop loss 20% hoga."*

## Rules
* The stop loss is **exactly -20% from the entry (market) price** for every strategy and universe, first_pullback
  included (its pullback-low stop is gone). Paper positions store `sl = entry x 0.80`.
* New strategy versions were created once (`migrate_v113`): `sl_pct = 20` (and first_pullback `max_sl_pct = 20`),
  every other learned param kept, and the change is recorded in `/strategies`.
* **Locked:** with `EXIT_SL_PCT=20` (the default) the learner never tunes `sl_pct` / `max_sl_pct`, in run or classic
  mode. The exit policy also enforces -20% on positions and watches opened earlier with another SL.
* Unchanged from v1.1.2: TP +1000%; protected from a +15% peak (stop >= entry +2%, trail 10/15/25/30%); 50% sold at
  +30%; time stop only while unprotected; 24 h max hold; rug guard.

## Replay (honest)
`SL_MODE=v112|v113 python3 tools/analysis/replay_v112.py data/*.db`: the same 125 recorded setups and the same
v1.1.2 exits, with only the stop loss different. Open positions are marked at the last price, and a rug counts as -95%.
Outputs: `docs/V113_REPLAY_SL_v112.txt`, `docs/V113_REPLAY_SL20.txt`.

| Stop loss | win | avg / trade | sum | stop-loss exits | trail exits | rugs | avg losing trade |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1.1.2 per strategy (8-15%) | 46.4% | -2.48% | -310 | 44 | 34 | 18 | -26.3% |
| **v1.1.3 fixed 20%** | **57.6%** | **-2.31%** | -288 | 19 | 44 | 21 | -36.7% |

* The wider stop lets many trades survive the first dip and recover (stop-loss exits 44 -> 19, win rate +11 pts).
* Each loss is bigger, though, and a few more trades ride into a rug, so the average improves only slightly
  (-2.48% -> -2.31%).
* In the >=$1M + gates subset (what the bot trades) there are only 7 setups: +1.86% -> +1.74%. Too few to judge.
* With the old v1.1.1 TP levels, SL 20% would have averaged -0.41% (win 64.8%), so the let-winners-run exits are
  still the bigger cost on this data. No recorded coin went above ~+145%.
